How to interpret the results

Data and denominators matter

Many projects use 42 label rows representing only 40 unique videos: two byte-identical pairs. Forty-two-row evaluations commonly contain 22 positive and 20 clean rows; deduplicated evaluations use 21 positive and 19 clean videos. Reusing these clips for fitting, rule selection and evaluation measures development performance.

Training-set accuracy, crop AUC, gesture F1, trigger recall, test-set F1 and mock test counts measure different things. They cannot form one leaderboard.

Check the whole decision

A timestamp near an annotation does not prove the correct event type or supporting rule. Several evaluators take the closest prediction without one-to-one matching and omit missed events from timecode error.

Some experiments get high recall or F1 by predicting every clip positive. Others get perfect precision by detecting very few positives. Failures and abstentions must stay in the denominator.

What the approaches suggest

  • Conservative hybrid pipelines: AliNurym, rsduv and Hotel-zulu report zero false alarms on their familiar clean sets, but miss roughly 29–36% of positive videos. This is a useful operating point to study, not proof of zero future false positives.
  • Recall-focused VLM pipelines: Veriti v12 catches 21/22 positive rows but flags 10/20 clean rows. Its larger reviewer reduces overall accuracy compared with its lighter v11. A larger model alone does not resolve the task.
  • Component learning: CompVision’s crop-state ablations and CashGuard’s setup-grouped state classifiers show more targeted learning than generic video-level classifiers. Their component scores still need a frozen end-to-end unseen-video test.
  • Small internal tests expose weak discrimination: Tumo’s seven-video test scores 28.6% accuracy; CashReview’s eight-video test flags everything; KFM’s 13-video internal holdout is also all-positive. Song-ev’s historical 16-video holdout is 56.25%. These separate studies do not establish a cross-project ranking.
  • Operations and recognition are distinct: several projects have real queues, authentication, evidence JPEGs and local model serving while detection is unqualified, disabled or empty. Software test counts and mock timings do not establish model accuracy.
