# Extended public-law coverage audit

**17 October 2026, Asia/Qyzylorda.** Public-source and CPU research only. No private clip, frame, label, annotation or model observation was read; no inference, GPU operation, deployment or catalogue mutation occurred.

The four default cash-rule branches are a genuine coverage limit. Improving their visual recall cannot detect a category absent from their facts and rule bindings. This audit found an important omitted provision on personal items and recording-capable communication devices: **National Bank Resolution No. 231, paragraph 12**. It also identifies additional cash-procedure branches and two independently supported workplace/fire-safety categories. None is enabled by this report. An expanded catalogue still cannot establish exhaustive hidden-test or legal coverage.

## Important correction: personal items and camera-capable communication devices

Current **No. 231 paragraph 12**, as revised by No. 71 of 10 November 2025, requires employees' personal belongings, cash and valuables to be stored outside the cash node; it permits bringing medicines, medical devices and communication equipment **without photo/video recording functions**. The clause is present in the already frozen and live-matching source at `rules/sources/nbk_231_current.txt:180`. This is an omitted public provision, not a new legal change. The earlier broad claim that no personal-object/phone-related source was verified is incomplete. [Official No. 231 source](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019680).

There is still no justified rule that every phone-shaped object, every phone use, every bag or every casual item is a violation. The lawful exceptions and distinction between a cash node, a public lobby, bank equipment and personal storage matter.

| Proposed category | Required visible/context facts | Ambiguity and exceptions | Safe implementation decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PERSONAL_BELONGINGS_STORED_IN_CASH_NODE`, No. 231 **12, first part** | Confirmed regulated-bank cash-node scope; worker established by observed cash duties/body continuity; an actual storage episode; affirmative provenance that the stored item is a personal belonging rather than bank operational equipment or client property; original support before/after placement | Wearing clothing is not automatically storing it. An unknown bag may contain permitted medicines/medical devices. Cash ownership cannot be inferred from position or a pocket. A workstation setting alone does not classify every visible room as a cash node. | **Real source gap, conditional implementation.** Emit a possible finding only when personal-item provenance and cash-node scope are supported; otherwise retain a neutral object observation. No blanket bags/clothing classifier. |
| `RECORDING_CAPABLE_COMM_DEVICE_BROUGHT_INTO_CASH_NODE`, No. 231 **12, second part**, read with its personal-item rule | Confirmed cash-node boundary; observed worker carries a handheld communication device into it; recording capability established by a clearly distinguishable camera/known device feature or an actually observed camera operation; same body/device tracked without face identification | A rectangular object or generic handset is insufficient. A radio or basic handset without a camera may be allowed. Bank CCTV is not a worker's carried personal communication device and No. 14 separately requires surveillance equipment. Treatment of bank-owned operational communication equipment must not be guessed. | **High-priority gated research branch.** Preserve the precise carried-device/recording-capability distinction and bounded possible-sign wording. The existing 19 facts cannot express this category; it needs a versioned extension and negative examples of permitted devices. |

Do not OCR or expose private communication contents to establish device type. Neither category requires personal identity, motive, account data or a disciplinary conclusion.

## Implementation recommendation

1. Preserve the default nineteen-axis/checkpoint identity while restoring and measuring recall for the already enabled branches. The observed screening-only shortcut and representational/localization defects are separate from taxonomy gaps.
2. Prioritize paragraph 12's precise device/personal-storage scope and the four already isolated experimental cash branches. Add incomplete-coin issue and client-present issue-count conditions only with explicit procedure tracking and full opportunity.
3. Put any additions into a separately versioned vocabulary/catalogue, with fixed Russian templates, exact source archives, exception gates and actual positive/negative visibility tests. Never relabel an old head's indices or treat a model's free text as a legal source.
4. Add tobacco/egress categories only if the product intentionally includes general workplace safety and those contexts are actually visible. Keep collection, vault and structural checks in corresponding scene profiles. No private-video occurrence or accuracy claim follows from this research.

The review corrected a real missed clause and found additional supported categories. It does not establish which user-tested clips contain those categories, nor justify automatically marking every phone, bag, open door or other person as a violation.

## Separate optional profile prepared after this review

Root authorized a narrow new profile for paragraph 12's two carefully gated cases and clear indoor workplace tobacco smoking. It is implemented in [the explicit loader](../../rules/workplace_catalog.py), [separate catalogue](../../rules/workplace_catalog.v1.json), [separate taxonomy](../../annotations/workplace-taxonomy.v1.json) and [profile instructions](../../rules/workplace_README.md). The default catalogue/taxonomy, original nineteen-axis files and production selection are unchanged.

The new profile has three branches and thirteen new facts; its **32-axis identity preserves the exact original nineteen-axis prefix**. Explicit `include_baseline=True` composes seven branches without changing any default loader or configuration. Original legal sources, clause excerpts, branch gates, exceptions, vocabulary and baseline hashes are validated; an altered profile fails closed. The original production loader rejects the new raw schema. The newly archived Health Code source includes exact official content/history responses and an explicitly derived metadata record. The public source manifest distinguishes HTTP-response hashes from extracted-text hashes.

The focused optional-profile suite reports **91 passing synthetic tests**, covering each required gate missing/unclear/opposite, actual action anchors, permitted-device/medical exceptions, unknown rectangles/bags, operational equipment, client/lobby scope, ordinary clothing, pen/inhaler/vapor/unlit tobacco/outdoor lookalikes, source tampering, deterministic regeneration and strict 32-fact physical prompt compatibility. These tests supply explicit generated fact states rather than pixels; they do not establish that a model recognizes those facts accurately. No private inference or profile activation occurred. Fire, collection and ATM proposals remain report-only.
