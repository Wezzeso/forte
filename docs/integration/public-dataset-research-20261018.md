# Public data for specialist pretraining — 18 October 2026

Use **KZ-BD paper-currency boxes plus MIDV-Holo artificial ID-card templates/frames** for the first small detector experiment. Add a capped, individually licensed Open Images cup subset only if acquisition and review fit the remaining window. Public data can teach objects; it does not supply bank transaction roles, absence-of-check intervals, cash-stack counts or a tiny key left in this safe.

This is primary-source research and an acquisition recommendation. It does not claim a completed training run, qualifying object metrics, a deployed new detector or improved Forte accuracy. The accepted VLM service stays unchanged while the separate candidate is trained and evaluated. The current account's 18 October **11:01:25 Asia/Qyzylorda** stop remains a hard boundary.

## Current tracking candidates

The official Google DeepMind [TAPNet repository](https://github.com/google-deepmind/tapnet/blob/main/README.md) now explicitly licenses its released TAPIR, BootsTAPIR, TAPNext and TAPNext++ checkpoints under Apache-2.0; RGB-Stacking/RoboTAP videos and TAP-Vid annotations have CC BY 4.0 terms. This provides a candidate for object-point continuity without relying on the original noncommercial CoTracker3 checkpoint. TAPVid-3D and third-party source videos have separate terms. RoboTAP's 265-video benchmark is described as evaluation-only; keep benchmark examples sealed when measuring it. These trackers are **not installed or validated in Forte** and point motion does not establish a bank action, physical contact or complete visibility.

[RH20T's author license](https://rh20t.github.io/#license) permits commercial use only for the `scene_0001`–`scene_0005` subset under CC BY-SA 4.0; `scene_0006`–`scene_0010` is noncommercial, including models trained on it. It offers robot/human manipulation sequences, not exact banknote counting, key-left or authenticity-check labels. Its large archives and incidental people/voices make it a later, selectively admitted source rather than this two-class training run. No procedural targets should be invented from task names.

The separate [public action-data review](public-action-dataset-research-20261018.md) covers procedural transfer candidates and their access/license limits. Those sources remain separate from the detector's object-label contract.

## Split and annotation contract

1. Freeze selected source versions, licenses, raw archive/annotation hashes, class map, importer, transforms, seed and split manifest **before fitting**. Verify received files against publisher digests; reject unsafe archive paths and corrupt or unmatched image/label pairs.
2. Use the preregistered KZ denomination split: assign training paper 500/1,000/2,000/5,000 and coins 1/2/5/10, development paper 10,000 and coins 20/50, sealed test paper 20,000 and coins 100/200. Training uses only published training images of its denominations; their original published validation images are excluded. Whole development/test denominations follow the declared policy independently of the author's split. No metrics influenced this choice. The later strict merge quarantine removed the entire 5000-tenge group; final counts and the verified frozen lock are in [the training result ledger](public-training-results-20261018.md). Quarantine exact SHA-256 and near-duplicate clusters spanning roles; perceptual hashes alone do not prove no shared scene/template.
3. Group all MIDV clips, frames, scans, templates and counterfeit/composite variants by physical/mock document identity and design. Reserve whole designs where possible. All children of one template inherit its split. For KZ, preserve any actual capture/physical-note group metadata; when missing, say grouping is unknown rather than inventing IDs. Repeated denominations in train/test are not evidence of unseen-design generalization.
4. Normalize public labels accurately: denomination boxes can become generic banknote boxes; document quadrangles can become their axis-aligned enclosing boxes. Known-transform template composites have synthetic boxes and synthetic provenance, not human scene labels. Keep ID cards separate from passport pages. No denomination/quad annotation creates a stack count, verified negative, hand contact, temporal omission or legal verdict.
5. Organizer/jury videos and every derivative stay outside public pretraining, synthetic backgrounds, crop mining, calibration and model selection. The already-used 40 supplied clips are a historical development regression suite, not a fresh untouched test. Do not iterate weights/prompts/thresholds against its errors. Select checkpoints on public development data, freeze once, then report a regression comparison; failure remains a result rather than a tuning instruction.

## Practical six-hour experiment

The initial recommendation below preceded the frozen export. Actual execution uses the 68 author-annotated Holo scene frames and retained KZ paper/coin images, with no generated template-composition expansion or Open Images additions. Exact preparation counts and current fit state are in [the result ledger](public-training-results-20261018.md).

The initial proposal was one compact **two-class banknote/ID-card** run: a balanced 500–1,500 KZ training images, eligible Holo ID-card templates transformed into 500–1,000 compositions, and a capped public object-confounder set only after its licenses/labels were admitted. These were proposed caps, not acquired counts. Any later compositions need disjoint document designs/background sources and separate real-scene/synthetic metrics; synthetic holdout success is not CCTV validation.

Use the existing pinned RF-DETR recipe with a supported small input, batch size one, accumulation and mixed precision; freeze the backbone for the first probe if the pinned implementation supports it. Run one bounded GPU-memory/step smoke before committing. Approximately 7 GB spare beside serving is a hard resource constraint, not proof that a training configuration fits. Cap memory, stop on serving pressure/deadline, and never restart/reconfigure the accepted VLM to make training fit. Reserve time for inference metrics and artifact hashing rather than running multiple untracked models.

Only public development metrics select the checkpoint; the sealed public test remains outside selection. Public pretraining does not satisfy the own-camera or full-video promotion gates. Until those pass, even a selected public checkpoint cannot enable ID, key, recount, detector-contact or partial-handover violations. A measured optimizer update and exported weights demonstrate training execution; they do not establish improved jury accuracy.
